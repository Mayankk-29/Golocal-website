From “I need help” to done

  1. Post it

    Add a title, category, short description, area and budget. Choose Normal or Insta. Quick Jobs fill in the common ones for you.

  2. Get messages

    Neighbours open your listing, see the area and budget, and tap Message Poster to talk details directly in the app.

  3. Get it done

    Agree on the work, finish the job, and keep your activity in one place on your profile.

How do I post a job on GoLocal?

Tap Post, enter a title, category, description, area and budget, choose Normal Job or Insta Job, and post. Accurate details help people nearby understand what you need.

How do I find someone to help with a job?

Browse listings, narrow them with category filters, and open a listing to see its description, location and budget. Tap Message Poster to contact the person who posted it.

What is the difference between Normal and Insta Jobs?

Normal Jobs stay active for 24 hours. Insta Jobs stay active for 10 minutes and are meant for urgent tasks.

What should I include when posting?

A clear title, the closest category, a short description of the task, the area and your budget.
